Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your Brand ("we," "us") operates a Discord community and associated social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube). This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you purchase access to our Discord community through LaunchPass, we (via LaunchPass and Discord) receive your email address, Discord username, and payment confirmation. We do not process or store your payment card details ourselves — that's handled directly by LaunchPass's payment processor.

If you interact with our social media accounts (comments, messages), we may see your public profile information and whatever you send us directly.

How we use it

We use your information to: grant and manage your access to the Discord community, respond to support questions, and communicate with you about your membership. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Third parties

We use LaunchPass (payment and access management) and Discord (community platform). Each has its own privacy policy governing data they process on our behalf.

Your choices

You can request the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us through Discord or the contact method listed on our storefront.

Contact

Questions about this policy — reach out via our Discord server or social media accounts.
